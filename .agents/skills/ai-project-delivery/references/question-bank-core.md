# Core stakeholder question bank — 120 questions

Mark each question as `Confirmed`, `Assumed`, `Unknown`, `Delegated`, or `N/A`. This is the core coverage; brownfield projects can answer using evidence from the repository and then confirm only conflicts.

## A. Context and Conception (Q-C001–Q-C012)

1. `Q-C001` If you could only describe the project idea in one sentence, what would it be?
2. `Q-C002` What specific event, observation, or requirement triggered this idea?
3. `Q-C003` Why is this problem worth solving right now?
4. `Q-C004` What will happen if we do nothing in the next 6–12 months?
5. `Q-C005` Who is currently most affected by this problem?
6. `Q-C006` What previous attempts have been made to solve this, and what were the outcomes?
7. `Q-C007` Are there existing products/processes you want to model this after, and what exactly is worth copying?
8. `Q-C008` What aspects of existing solutions make you dissatisfied?
9. `Q-C009` Is this a new product, a replacement for an old system, or an addition to an existing system?
10. `Q-C010` What is the initial geographic scope, business unit, or target market?
11. `Q-C011` What are the primary language and cultural context for this product?
12. `Q-C012` One year from now, how would you tell this success story?

## B. Problem and Root Cause (Q-C013–Q-C024)

13. `Q-C013` Retell the last time this problem occurred, step-by-step in real life.
14. `Q-C014` Who first discovered the problem and how did they react?
15. `Q-C015` How often does this problem occur?
16. `Q-C016` How much time, money, or opportunity is lost each time it occurs?
17. `Q-C017` What evidence proves that this problem actually exists?
18. `Q-C018` What are the visible symptoms, and what root cause do you suspect?
19. `Q-C019` Which causes have been verified, and which are only hypotheses?
20. `Q-C020` How does the problem differ across user groups?
21. `Q-C021` In what cases does the problem NOT occur, and why?
22. `Q-C022` Are there any manual workarounds hiding the true scale of the problem?
23. `Q-C023` What lies outside the control of this project?
24. `Q-C024` If we could only solve part of the problem, which part would create the most value?

## C. Stakeholders and Decision Rights (Q-C025–Q-C036)

25. `Q-C025` Who is the sponsor holding ultimate accountability?
26. `Q-C026` Who is the product owner deciding priorities and product behavior?
27. `Q-C027` Who uses the system daily but has no purchasing/approval authority?
28. `Q-C028` Who will operate, support, or troubleshoot the system after handover?
29. `Q-C029` Who owns the data being processed?
30. `Q-C030` Who is responsible for security, privacy, and compliance?
31. `Q-C031` Who could block the project even if they do not directly use it?
32. `Q-C032` Which third parties or vendors must participate or approve?
33. `Q-C033` When stakeholders disagree, who has final decision rights?
34. `Q-C034` What is the expected response/turnaround time for each approver?
35. `Q-C035` What is the primary communication channel and the desired review schedule?
36. `Q-C036` Who needs to receive what reports, and at what frequency?

## D. Users and Context of Use (Q-C037–Q-C048)

37. `Q-C037` Who are the different user groups?
38. `Q-C038` What is the primary goal of each user group when using the system?
39. `Q-C039` What are the digital literacy and business knowledge levels of each group?
40. `Q-C040` What devices, operating systems, and browsers do they use?
41. `Q-C041` Where do they use it: office, field, home, on the move, or in weak network areas?
42. `Q-C042` What is the frequency and duration of each user session?
43. `Q-C043` Are there any peak times or seasonal factors?
44. `Q-C044` What specific accessibility needs do the users have?
45. `Q-C045` Are minors, elderly, or vulnerable groups using the system?
46. `Q-C046` What trust factors or expectations do users have before entering data or paying?
47. `Q-C047` What reasons would cause them to refuse the system or revert to old methods?
48. `Q-C048` Who represents users well enough to participate in usability testing/UAT?

## E. Outcomes and Metrics (Q-C049–Q-C060)

49. `Q-C049` What is the most critical business outcome?
50. `Q-C050` What is the most critical user outcome?
51. `Q-C051` What is the current baseline of each metric?
52. `Q-C052` What is the target to achieve, unit of measure, and deadline?
53. `Q-C053` What leading indicators alert us early that we are on the right track?
54. `Q-C054` What lagging indicators confirm real value?
55. `Q-C055` What guardrail metrics must not degrade during optimization?
56. `Q-C056` What data source is used to measure metrics, and who trusts this source?
57. `Q-C057` How often will metrics be reviewed?
58. `Q-C058` What represents minimum success, target success, and outstanding success?
59. `Q-C059` Under what conditions will we halt, pivot, or rollback the product?
60. `Q-C060` Who signs off that outcomes have been achieved?

## F. Scope and Priorities (Q-C061–Q-C072)

61. `Q-C061` What are the three mandatory capabilities of the first release?
62. `Q-C062` What is explicitly out of scope?
63. `Q-C063` What hypothesis must the MVP prove?
64. `Q-C064` What can be done manually in the initial phase?
65. `Q-C065` Which capabilities depend on other capabilities?
66. `Q-C066` Which items are high-value but low-risk?
67. `Q-C067` Which items are high-risk needing an early spike?
68. `Q-C068` Will priority be decided using MoSCoW, WSJF, RICE, or direct authority?
69. `Q-C069` Which deadlines are fixed and which are negotiable?
70. `Q-C070` Are there legal, contractual, marketing, or operational milestones?
71. `Q-C071` If you had to cut 30% of the scope, what would you drop first?
72. `Q-C072` If you had 30% more time, what is the best additional value we could deliver?

## G. Current and Future Processes (Q-C073–Q-C084)

73. `Q-C073` What trigger starts the business process?
74. `Q-C074` What are the current steps in their exact sequence?
75. `Q-C075` Who executes and who approves each step?
76. `Q-C076` What data/documents enter and what are the outputs of each step?
77. `Q-C077` Where are the wait times, bottlenecks, and manual handovers?
78. `Q-C078` What rules cause a case to branch to another path?
79. `Q-C079` What are the most common exceptions?
80. `Q-C080` When can a case be cancelled, edited, reverted, or reopened?
81. `Q-C081` What states need to be saved, and who is allowed to change states?
82. `Q-C082` What evidence/audit trail is required for each decision?
83. `Q-C083` What steps in the future process should be eliminated, automated, or kept?
84. `Q-C084` If the system goes down, what is the fallback process?

## H. Business Model and Rules (Q-C085–Q-C096)

85. `Q-C085` Who pays or funds this product?
86. `Q-C086` Where does direct and indirect financial value come from?
87. `Q-C087` Is there a model for pricing, fees, quotas, credits, refunds, or commissions?
88. `Q-C088` Which calculation rules must be absolutely precise?
89. `Q-C089` How are taxes, currencies, exchange rates, and rounding handled?
90. `Q-C090` Are there limitations based on plans, roles, markets, or contracts?
91. `Q-C091` Which rules change frequently and who is allowed to edit them?
92. `Q-C092` Which rules must have date-based or version-based validity?
93. `Q-C093` What data source resolves disputes?
94. `Q-C094` Is four-eyes approval or segregation of duties required?
95. `Q-C095` Which fraud or abuse behaviors are of greatest concern?
96. `Q-C096` Which business decisions must be explainable to users or auditors?

## I. Constraints, Dependencies, and Resources (Q-C097–Q-C108)

97. `Q-C097` What is the expected build and operational budget?
98. `Q-C098` What are the roles, capabilities, and availability of the current team?
99. `Q-C099` What technologies or vendors must be used?
100. `Q-C100` What technologies or vendors are forbidden?
101. `Q-C101` Which systems, APIs, data, or teams are dependencies?
102. `Q-C102` Which dependencies lack commitments on timeline/SLA?
103. `Q-C103` Are there hosting, data residency, or network constraints?
104. `Q-C104` Are there contracts or licenses limiting design options?
105. `Q-C105` Who will provide sample data, test environments, and credentials?
106. `Q-C106` What working hours, time zones, and holiday schedules affect delivery?
107. `Q-C107` What organizational or political risks could affect adoption?
108. `Q-C108` Which assumption, if wrong, would disrupt the plan the most?

## J. Acceptance, Change, and Handover (Q-C109–Q-C120)

109. `Q-C109` Who accepts each capability, and how?
110. `Q-C110` What criteria would cause a release to be rejected?
111. `Q-C111` What defect severities are allowed to remain open at release?
112. `Q-C112` What evidence do stakeholders want to see before approving?
113. `Q-C113` When a change is requested, who analyzes the impact and who approves it?
114. `Q-C114` How are urgent/emergency changes handled differently?
115. `Q-C115` What documentation is mandatory for users, operations, and auditing?
116. `Q-C116` Who will receive the code, data, infrastructure, and knowledge transfer post-project?
117. `Q-C117` How long does post-go-live support last, and under what SLA?
118. `Q-C118` What knowledge currently lives only in one person's head?
119. `Q-C119` What conditions confirm the project is officially closed?
120. `Q-C120` Is there anything important I haven't asked that you believe will decide success or failure?
